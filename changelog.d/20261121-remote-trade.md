### Bots buy and sell at the auction house from afar

Random bots now trade at their faction's auction house without walking there: once an hour a bot buys the best upgrade it can afford and wears it, and every two hours it lists one or two items, at the usual price, when the house pays more than a vendor (`AiPlayerbot.CoaEconomy.RemoteBuy.*`, `AiPlayerbot.CoaEconomy.RemoteSell.*`). These are real auctions: players can buy what the bots list, and a player's listing bought by a bot pays its seller. Never a player's own bots or alts, never in a fight, a dungeon or a battleground.
