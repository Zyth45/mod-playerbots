### Gear history of each character
The server now keeps the last 20 gear events of every character, players and bots alike, in the new table `coa_item_event` of the characters database, for the dashboard's character sheet: each piece worn (green or better, with where it came from), each enchantment put on by an enchanting spell or scroll or by a bot enchanter, and one line for each pass of a bot's auto-gear with the number of pieces it changed. The same piece worn again within a minute counts once; logging in writes nothing. On by default: `AiPlayerbot.CoaWorld.ItemEvent.Enable = 0` turns it off. CoA mystic enchantments are not recorded yet.

### Crafted pieces in the armory
A piece made by a profession, or signed by its maker, now shows its origin in the armory: `selfcraft` when its maker wears it, `craft` when someone else does (a trade, a mail order).

### Every piece worn shows where it came from
A piece looted in a dungeon now shows as dungeon loot, and one looted in a raid as raid loot, group rolls included (battlegrounds and arenas count as open world). The armory also tells a piece received by mail, traded, taken from the guild bank, won in an invasion or bought with honor, and the tools and gear a bot is given. Only pieces worn before the update stay of unknown origin. Grey and white pieces are not tracked. `AiPlayerbot.CoaWorld.ItemEvent.Keep` (default `20`) sets how many gear events each character keeps.
