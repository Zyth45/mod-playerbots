### Low-level bots shop at the auction house too
A bot needed too much gold before it went shopping, so bots below level 20 almost never did. The threshold drops from 500 to 200 copper of spending money per level (`AiPlayerbot.CoaEconomy.BotAuctions.Shop.MinCopperPerLevel`): a level 15 bot now goes with about 1.5 gold in its bags. A value you changed yourself stays.
