### Bots ride from level 5, each on a mount of its own
As in CoA, bots now ride from level 5 with Novice Riding (40%), then Apprentice Riding at 20, instead of walking until 20 (AiPlayerbot.UseGroundMountAtMinLevel = 5; a value you changed yourself is kept). Each bot keeps a ground mount of its own among those it knows, instead of nearly all of them riding the same elekk.
