### Bots shop at the auction house

Bots went to the auction house only to sell, so most of them never bought anything there. A bot with gold enough now also goes there to buy an upgrade or a bag, at most once an hour (`AiPlayerbot.CoaEconomy.BotAuctions.Shop.*`). A bot stopping by an auctioneer in a capital no longer steps away before its visit is done, which made the visit do nothing.
