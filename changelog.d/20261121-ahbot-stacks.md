### Auction house bot: stacks of a believable size
The auction house bot no longer lists whole CoA stacks of up to 1000 (980 Elementium Shards in one lot). Its stacks are now capped as in the original game: 20 for trade goods, consumables, reagents, gems and the like, 200 for arrows and bullets. The stocked crafting materials follow the same cap. A cap you changed yourself stays.
