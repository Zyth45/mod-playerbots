title: Bot enchanters charge a little more for their work
---
A bot that has an enchanter bot enchant its gear now pays a fee of at least 80 copper per level of the customer, up from 50: about 22 silver at level 27, on top of the reagents. Enchanters earn more for their work, while customers still pay less than a green piece of the same level costs at the auction house, and nearly every customer can still afford it. A realm that kept the old default (`AiPlayerbot.CoaEconomy.EnchantOrders.MinFeePerLevel = 50`) moves to 80 with the update; a value you set yourself stays.
