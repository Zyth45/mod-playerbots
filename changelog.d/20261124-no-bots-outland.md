### No more bots on the Exodar, Silvermoon or Northrend
Random bots were sent to the Blood Elves' and Draenei's isles (the Exodar, Silvermoon and the zones around) and could reach Northrend, where CoA has nothing for them. They are no longer sent there, and those already there go back to their hearth on the old continents, or to their race's start.
