### Bots no longer have or advertise Inscription or Jewelcrafting
A player on a classic realm saw bots in Trade talk about Inscription and Jewelcrafting, professions the realm does not offer: Conquest of Azeroth's live realm has no Inscription, and on a classic realm Jewelcrafting is taught only in lands that stay closed. Bots are no longer given either profession. They no longer advertise them, craft their goods or prospect ore. They also no longer put up for sale the items only these professions make: glyphs, inks, milled pigments, cut gems, jewelcrafted jewelry, and the Designs and Techniques no monster drops. What the world itself gives stays on sale: scrolls looted from monsters, gems found while mining, looted Designs. Players and their professions are not affected.

Existing random bots that had one of these professions unlearn it, with its recipes, wherever they are once they are online and out of combat, and take up another profession the usual way, keeping two main professions. At most 30 bots change each minute, so 3,000 bots are done in under two hours and a busy realm does not see them all change at once. Their auctions already up are left to run out.
