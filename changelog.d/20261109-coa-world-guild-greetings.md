title: Your guild's bots say hello back
---
With AiPlayerbot.CoaWorld.Guilds.Greetings (off by default), when you say hello in guild chat ("hi", "hey", "bonjour", "re"...), one to three online guildmates answer after a few seconds, and one or two greet you when you log in. Never all of them at once, and not again within ten minutes.
