### Bots chat back by whisper, and know when to stop
- Random bots answer more of the small talk a player whispers them ("how are you", "what are you doing", "where are you"...), in English and French, from the new lines of the CoA Live bank.
- A "how are you" asked back gets a "fine", never another "how are you".
- After five to eight answers, a bot gives a plausible excuse that fits what it is doing ("sorry, my BG just popped", "dungeon queue, gotta go", busy in a fight or a group, away at low level), then stays quiet for that player for 20 to 40 minutes; insisting does not make it longer. Option: AiPlayerbot.CoaLive.WhisperEscape.Enable (on by default; off with "Bot chat").
- A craft quote a player asks an artisan by whisper is answered even with "Bot chat" off.
