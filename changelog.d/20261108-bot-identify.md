title: The SquidBots addon knows bots without the "Bot" surname
---
With `AiPlayerbot.CoaBotIdentify = 1`, or whenever `AiPlayerbot.CoaBotSurname` is not 1, the bots of your group answer the SquidBots Lite addon on a hidden addon channel ("WHO?" / "IAM"), so the addon still knows who is a bot when bots carry no "Bot" surname. Only members of your group and faction get an answer. Off by default.
