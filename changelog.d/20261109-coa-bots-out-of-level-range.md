title: No more level 60 bots wandering in starting zones
---
Bots that duelled in front of Stormwind or Orgrimmar now always go back where they came from, even when they could not leave at once, instead of staying behind and walking around Elwynn Forest or Durotar. With the CoA World options, the bots logged in near you are of a level for your zone, and a bot far above the zone it walks in (a level 55 in Elwynn) is sent to a place of its level, out of your sight.
