### Alts can be sent on missions to Outland
Gathering missions now cover Outland: an alt can mine Fel Iron Ore in Hellfire Peninsula or Zangarmarsh, and an order
for an item found only there (`mission order Fel Iron Ore` in Stormwind) teleports it over. Rescaled zones count at
their level 60 creatures. Random bots keep their own maps (AiPlayerbot.RandomBotMaps); the missions' maps are set by
AiPlayerbot.Missions.Maps (default 0,1,530).
