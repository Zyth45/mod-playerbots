### CoA Bots 3.2
- **Bots land when they leave a flight**: a bot that ends a flight or a fly mount high up drops to the ground under it, instead of standing in the air.
- **Bank visits end outside**: bots sent to a city banker arrive on the ground before the bank, no longer on its roof or in a wall.
- **No dead-end paths in the capitals**: a city path that stops short of its goal is refused, and a bot wandering to an NPC it cannot reach gives up on it.
- **Self-found rolls**: self-found bots roll need on what is an upgrade for them in dungeons, and the dungeon simulation gives its loot to the member it upgrades (`AiPlayerbot.CoaSelfFound.Rolls`).
- **Level brackets 50-59** filled as the others (`CoaLevelBracketRules`).
- **Guild charters signed again**: bots read the signatures the way the core now stores them.
- **Crafting and enchanting for themselves**: once an hour in a capital, a bot studies what it could craft or enchant for itself and buys the reagents it lacks, a vendor's first, the auction house's next, under a gold cap and only when the gain is worth it (`AiPlayerbot.CoaEconomy.CraftForSelf.*`, `EnchantForSelf.*`, `SelfStudy.*`).
- **No more bot accounts at each start**: accounts of another prefix are no longer counted, so a start does not create accounts again.
- **`.squidbots why`** (game master accounts): what a selected bot is doing, in the chat and in `squidbots_why.log` to send us (`AiPlayerbot.CoaWhy.Enable`).
