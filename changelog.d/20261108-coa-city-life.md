title: City Life: capitals full of bots at the inn, the bank, the auction house and the forge
---
With `AiPlayerbot.CoaLive.City.Enable = 1`, a random bot visiting a capital now and then takes a station instead of a plain NPC. It sits at the inn, has a word with a banker or an auctioneer, hammers at an anvil or a forge, or strikes a training dummy, and stays a few minutes there. At most 3 bots share a station.
