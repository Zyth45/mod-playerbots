title: Guild gear gifts fit your role, and you choose to accept them
---
The gear a guildmate sends you now fits your role (no caster weapon for a warrior, no strength piece for a mage), with its random suffix counted, and a two-hand weapon is weighed against both hands. With AiPlayerbot.CoaWorld.Guilds.GearAsk (off by default), the gift is first offered: whisper yes or no to the bot, or use the SquidBots Lite Gifts view; it is mailed only if you accept, and a refused item is not offered again for 30 days.
