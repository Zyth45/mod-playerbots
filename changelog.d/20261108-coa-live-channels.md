title: Bots talk in General, Trade, LookingForGroup and guild chat (CoA Live)
---
With `AiPlayerbot.CoaLive.Enable = 1`, random bots also talk in channels with real players in them, at a human pace:
- General chat of your zone;
- Trade in capitals: they sell items they really carry and look for real auctions, at real auction prices;
- LookingForGroup: a real dungeon of their level and the roles still missing (whisper them to join);
- guild chat: hello when you log in, goodbye, gz on a level up, and a word on a nice loot.

Quiet channels stay quiet.
