### Artisans quote what they advertise

- An artisan bot whispered the link of an item it can craft ("how much for [item]", "price [item]", "wts?", "do you
  have [item]", or the link alone) now answers with its craft quote (materials on the artisan, cash on delivery)
  instead of the seller's "everything I sell is on the AH". Say yes and the piece comes by COD mail. An item it
  cannot craft keeps the usual answer.
- A quote the player asked for is always answered (no chance roll).
