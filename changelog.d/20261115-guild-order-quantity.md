### Guild orders keep the count asked for
- An order for 20 [Silver Bar] (guild chat or the addon's panel) now brings 20 bars, not one: the guildmate who crafts it casts the recipe as many times as needed (one stack at most) and asks only for the materials of those casts.
- In guild chat, write the count next to the link: "need 20 [Silver Bar]", "20x [Silver Bar]" or "[Silver Bar] x20" (1 to 20). Without a count, one craft is sent as before.
- A guildmate who has the whole count in its bags sends it first; one with fewer is asked only when nobody can craft it.
