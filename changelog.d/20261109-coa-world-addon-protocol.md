title: Guild orders and world events in the SquidBots Lite addon
---
With the SquidBots Lite World panel, a player can order an item from their guild's bots, see their orders of the last 7 days (shared, crafted, bought, none or refused, with the reason) and the gear the guild gave them, and follow the invasions, gold rushes and server firsts they can see in game. Off by default (AiPlayerbot.CoaWorld.Guilds.Addon, AiPlayerbot.CoaWorld.Events.Addon). Guild orders asked in guild chat that were dropped without a word are now kept with their reason.
