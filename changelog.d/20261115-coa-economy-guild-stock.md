### Bots keep a little stock for their guild

- Gathering bots now keep a few kinds of what they gather, of their level band: one cloth for everyone, plus ore and
  bars for miners, herbs for herbalists, leather for skinners. Artisans keep a stack of the intermediates they make
  from their own materials (bars, bolts of cloth, cured leather). Up to 2 stacks of each, bags and bank together;
  extra full stacks go to the bank when they visit the auction house.
- This stock is there for guild orders: a guildmate who asks for 20 bars may get some free from a member's stock.
- Artisans buying materials for their stock at the auction house now stay within a daily share per guild: only items
  with at least 8 auctions, never more than a quarter of them, at most 10 purchases a day per guild. Players always
  find materials.
- Option: AiPlayerbot.CoaEconomy.GuildStock.Enable (on by default) and its settings.
