title: Performance options for many bots (off by default)
---
Four new options to lighten the server with many bots, all off by default: bot packets handled in their map's thread (AiPlayerbot.CoaWorld.BotPacketsInMapThread), a 10-second cache of the zones with a real player (ActiveZones.Enable), a slower AI for asleep bots far from any player (IdleBotAI.Enable, IdleBotAI.IntervalMs) and SmartScale on the average update time (SmartScaleAverage.Enable).
