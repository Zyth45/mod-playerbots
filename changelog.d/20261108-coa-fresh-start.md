title: Launch of a new living realm: bots start at level 1 and level at their own pace
---
`AiPlayerbot.CoaFreshStart.Enable = 1` is set by the launcher's "New living server" and is meant for a new realm. Every random bot starts at the level it was made at and is never rolled to a higher level, so there is no level 60 on day one. Each bot levels at a pace of its own: a few fast ones, the mass, some late ones. Use it with the Solo self-found preset.
