title: Guild activities from the addon: gather, party of 5, guild raid
---
With AiPlayerbot.CoaWorld.Guilds.Activities (off by default) and the SquidBots Lite addon, your guild's bots can come and join you (gather), form a party of 5 for a dungeon, or a raid of 10, 15 or 25 with a balanced number of tanks and healers. One activity at a time, with a short cooldown.
