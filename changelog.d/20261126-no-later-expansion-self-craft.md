### Bots no longer craft Burning Crusade gear for themselves on a classic realm
A player saw a level 60 bot wearing a Fel Iron Chain Coif, an item level 90 Burning Crusade piece, on a classic realm. On a realm whose level cap is 60, bots no longer craft for themselves a recipe that needs a later expansion reagent, such as the Fel Iron Bars of the Fel Iron Chain Coif. Classic crafts stay, including those of Ahn'Qiraj and Naxxramas, such as the Obsidian Mail Tunic. A realm whose cap goes past 60 keeps its Burning Crusade and Wrath of the Lich King recipes. Pieces already worn stay until the bot's gear is renewed.
