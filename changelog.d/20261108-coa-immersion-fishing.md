title: Bots fish on the shores (CoA Live Immersion, off by default)
---
With `AiPlayerbot.CoaLive.Immersion.Enable = 1`, a random bot idling in the open world near water now and then goes to the shore and fishes for 4 to 15 minutes, then goes on with its life. It never does this in a group, a fight, an instance or a capital.
