title: Pets tab of the SquidBots addon: your alts' utility companions
---
Your alts now answer the Pets tab of SquidBots Lite (`pet list`, `pet set <loot|quest|class|craft> <spell id>`, `pet use <category|spell id>`, `pet dismiss`, `pet auto on|off`). The tab covers Lootbot 3000, the Kleptomanic Demon, the Wondrous Wisdomball, the Books of Ascension and of Conquest and the Book of Artisans. The loot favourite is the same setting as `loot pet`. The hunter `pet attack|follow|stay` commands are unchanged.
