### Livelier capitals
City Life bots now also read their mail at the mailboxes and sit on the benches and chairs of the city, never take the same kind of stop twice in a row, and now and then settle at the inn, a seat, a forge or a dummy for 20 to 60 minutes, so you meet the same faces at the same places (`AiPlayerbot.CoaLive.City.PostChancePct`, `PostMinMinutes`, `PostMaxMinutes`). Bots that know fishing sometimes fish at the city's water too (`AiPlayerbot.CoaLive.City.FishingPct`). On by default.
