title: Your bot guild answers your requests and equips you (option, off by default)
---
With `AiPlayerbot.CoaWorld.Guilds.Enable = 1`, ask your guild for an item in guild chat ("anyone have [item]?", "can someone craft [item]"). A guildmate who has one mails it for free; otherwise one who knows the recipe crafts it and mails it cash on delivery at the cost of the materials; otherwise one buys it at the auction house and mails it at the price paid (3 requests a day, up to rare items). Every few days, at login or when you level up, a guildmate may also mail you a piece of gear of your level that is clearly better than what you wear.
