### Bots arrive on the city's floor
A bot sent to a capital (to a banker, an innkeeper, a hub) could land on a small patch of floor cut off from the rest of the city, like by the Stormwind bank, and reach nothing from there. Each arrival point is now checked once against the city's walkable floor and moved a few yards onto it, or no longer used (AiPlayerbot.CoaArrivalCheck).
