title: Your guild's bots are online with you
---
With AiPlayerbot.CoaWorld.Guilds.PlayerGuildOnline (off by default) and the online/offline rotation of the bots, the bot members of your guild log in first while you are online, up to 40% of them (AiPlayerbot.CoaWorld.Guilds.PlayerGuildOnlinePercent), and log out last: your guild no longer looks empty.
