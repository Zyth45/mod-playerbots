title: Social bots: they raise you, help you, buff you and ask you to group (CoA Live)
---
With `AiPlayerbot.CoaLive.Social.Enable = 1` (and CoA Live on), a free random bot passing you can:
- raise you when you lie dead;
- help in a fight you are losing, with a heal or by attacking your enemy;
- cast one of its buffs on you;
- ask you to group when you are alone and close in level. This is rare: once in 30 minutes per player.

Bots also thank you when you buff them.
