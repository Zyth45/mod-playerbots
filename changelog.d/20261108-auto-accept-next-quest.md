title: Bots now get the quests that start by themselves after a turn-in (Rite of Vision and others)
---
Some quests start by themselves when you turn in the previous one, like the tauren Rites of the Earthmother in Camp Narache. Bots never received these, so every quest after them was out of their reach. Rite of Vision in Mulgore was one of them. Bots now get them as players do.
