title: Bots react like people around you (CoA Live, off by default)
---
With `AiPlayerbot.CoaLive.Enable = 1`, random bots greet you when you pass by, cheer your level up, bow after a duel, thank you for a resurrection, call the pull of a boss in your group, idle at the inn or in a capital, and answer your whispers after a human typing delay, sometimes with a typo or an abbreviation. Ask one to group and it may invite you. Lines and emotes come from the CoA Live bank, in English and French, and a bot only speaks when a real player can hear it.
