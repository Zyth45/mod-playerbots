title: Bots get a personality (CoA souls, off by default)
---
With `AiPlayerbot.CoaSoul.Enable = 1`, each random bot gets a personality: lazy, quester, wanderer, trader or PvP. It keeps it at every start. The lazy rest and visit the city more often, the traders crowd the city and its NPCs, the PvP go to world PvP and the wanderers roam and fly. With CoA Live, each bot also picks the lines that fit its personality and talks more or less. The share of each personality is set with `AiPlayerbot.CoaSoul.Weights`.
