### No more bots appearing at the starting points
The level-up guard of 3.3 did not cover the other ways a bot is teleported, and bench 331a still showed bots under level 6 appearing at their race's starting point. A bot the server revives after it stayed dead too long now rises where it lay if it is under level 6. Every teleport of a bot to a place of its level now follows the same rule as the level up: it never arrives within 150 yards of a player. That covers the periodic teleport, the teleport after 10 minutes without experience, the revive and the bankers. As before, it never leaves from within 150 yards of a player either. When every spot is seen, the bot stays put and tries again later. A bot sent to a starting area as a new character is unchanged. This is the same key: `AiPlayerbot.CoaLive.LevelupTeleportGuard = 0` turns it all off.
