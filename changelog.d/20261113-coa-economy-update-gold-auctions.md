title: Bots' gold brought down once to the new scale; dead auctions removed
---
Bots' gold is brought down once to the new scale; your characters and alts are not touched. At this update, each random bot of every realm keeps at most what the level scale (AiPlayerbot.CoaEconomy.BotLevelGold of that realm) pays up to its level: about 100 gold at level 60, 255 at 80; a poorer bot keeps what it has. Only random bots' own gold changes: players' characters and alts, guild banks, mail and auctions stay as they are. An empty scale leaves the bots' gold alone. The update also removes the auctions whose item no longer exists (left by bots deleted while they sold; nobody could see or buy them), a bid on one being refunded by mail. Both are done once per realm; "Undo the update" puts everything back.
