title: Bots level along credible routes (option, off by default)
---
With `AiPlayerbot.CoaWorld.Leveling.Enable = 1`, a bot moving on to a place of its level goes to a zone its faction levels in at that level (Westfall or Loch Modan for the Alliance at 10-20, the Barrens or Silverpine for the Horde, Stranglethorn for both at 30-45...), most often on the continent it is already on.
