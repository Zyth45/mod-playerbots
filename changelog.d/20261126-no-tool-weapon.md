### No more skinning knives as weapons
Bots no longer fight with a Skinning Knife or a Mining Pick in hand: a profession tool is never chosen as a weapon, and a bot holding one takes a real weapon from its bags again (after fishing too). The tools stay in the bags for gathering.
Bots that already held a tool put a real weapon back in hand within a minute of the update, from their bags or from their off hand.
