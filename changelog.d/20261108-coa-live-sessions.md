title: Bots come and go like players (CoA Live sessions, off by default)
---
With `AiPlayerbot.CoaLive.Sessions.Enable = 1`, each random bot stays online for a session of a believable length, from 15 minutes to a long evening. It then logs out for a while and another one takes its place. The number of bots online always stays the number you set. Bots in a group, a dungeon or a battleground never leave mid-way.
