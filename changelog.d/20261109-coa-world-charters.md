title: Bots sign guild charters and found guilds of their own
---
With AiPlayerbot.CoaWorld.Guilds.Charters (off by default), a random bot without a guild that you offer your guild charter signs it after a few seconds (now and then it says no), and asking aloud for signatures ("sign my charter") gets the guildless bots around you to sign. Now and then a guildless bot in a capital buys a charter, gathers signatures and founds its own guild. A share of the bots always stays without a guild (AiPlayerbot.CoaWorld.Guilds.UnguildedPercent, 30) so you can meet some.
