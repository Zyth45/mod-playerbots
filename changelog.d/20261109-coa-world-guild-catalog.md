title: Search what your guild's bots can send you
---
With AiPlayerbot.CoaWorld.Guilds.Catalog (off by default) and the SquidBots Lite addon, the Guild tab lists what your guild's online bots have in their bags or can craft (at the materials' cost), searchable by name, and orders it in one click.
