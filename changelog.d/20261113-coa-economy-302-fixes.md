title: A livelier auction house, artisans that tell you about your order, no cooking feats in bursts
---
The auction house's own seller lists again on realms with many bots (the bots' auctions no longer count against its quota), bringing back its epic weapons and armour. Bots keep at most 10 auctions of one item in a house, and 3 of one upgrade kit (no more 50 "Essence of Fire Upgrade Kit" at the top), settings AiPlayerbot.CoaEconomy.BotAuctions.MaxPerItem and MaxPerUpgradeKit. An artisan you order from by the addon now whispers when it mails your order (with its cash on delivery) and thanks you when you pay. Bots no longer learn cooking and first aid recipes in bulk ("has earned the achievement [Lunch Lady]" in bursts). The older "WTS [item] for [price]" that no quote stood behind is silent while the bots' living chat speaks: every bot WTS in Trade can now be answered.
