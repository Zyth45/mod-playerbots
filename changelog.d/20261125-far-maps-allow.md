### A switch to let the bots into Outland and Northrend
Since 3.2.2 I keep the random bots out of Outland, the Blood Elf and Draenei isles and Northrend. Some of you asked why. It stays that way by default, and there is now a switch to open those maps again: `AiPlayerbot.CoaLive.FarMaps.Allow = 1`. With it, the bots may go there by portal, boat, flight, hearthstone or quest, and stay there. With a level cap of 60, the bots are still never sent to level in Outland or Northrend, nor to the Exodar or Silvermoon, but the Blood Elf and Draenei zones (Eversong Woods, Ghostlands, Azuremyst and Bloodmyst Isles) become levelling spots again.
