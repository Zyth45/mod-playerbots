### Move audit: through the relief, inside walls, and where a move came from
The night bench of the bots' odd moves (AiPlayerbot.CoaMoveAudit, off by default) now also catches bots going through the relief outdoors and bots standing inside a wall or a building, says where each move came from (the core's path, a straight line, a teleport), and writes an hourly summary with samples to CoaBots.log.
