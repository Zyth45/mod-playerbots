### Bots hearth with you
When you use your hearthstone, your bots standing within 30 yards, out of combat and with their own hearthstone ready, cast theirs with you and arrive next to you a moment after you; their hearthstone goes on cooldown. A bot whose hearthstone is on cooldown stays where it is. On by default: `AiPlayerbot.CoaHearthWithMaster = 0` turns it off.
