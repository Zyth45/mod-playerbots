### Classic bags on Classic realms
On a realm capped at level 60, I no longer give every bot four Portable Holes, a Wrath of the Lich King bag: new bots get Traveler's Backpacks (16 slots), and an empty Portable Hole of an existing bot is swapped for one at its next login. A bag that holds anything is never touched, nothing in it is lost, and realms with a higher cap keep the Portable Hole.
